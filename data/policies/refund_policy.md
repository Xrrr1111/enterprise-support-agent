# Refund Policy

## Eligibility and timing

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days after confirmed delivery. Digital goods and personalized items are refundable only when defective or materially different from their description. A refund request for an undelivered order is reviewed against the order and shipping status.

## Processing

After an eligible returned item passes warehouse inspection, the refund is initiated within 2 business days. Banks and card networks may require an additional 3-7 business days to post the credit. Original shipping charges are not refundable unless the company shipped the wrong or defective item.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds may be issued for missing accessories, quantity discrepancies, or an agreed price adjustment. The support record must state the approved amount and reason. Refunds always return to the original payment method unless that method is no longer available, in which case a human agent must review the case.
