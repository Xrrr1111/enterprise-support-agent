# After-Sales Policy

## Warranty coverage

Hardware products include a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use. Accidental damage, unauthorized repair, cosmetic wear, and misuse are excluded unless an additional protection plan applies.

## Troubleshooting and repair

Support may request reasonable diagnostics, serial-number verification, photos, or logs before approving repair or replacement. Approved warranty repairs include parts and labor. If the product cannot be repaired within 15 business days after service-center receipt, support may offer an equivalent replacement.

## Escalation

Safety concerns, repeated repair failures, suspected account takeover, threatened legal action, and requests for exceptions to policy must be escalated to a human specialist. The ticket should include the customer request, prior troubleshooting, order identifier when available, and the reason for escalation.
