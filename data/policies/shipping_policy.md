# Shipping Policy

## Handling and delivery estimates

Paid in-stock orders are normally handed to the carrier within 2 business days. Standard delivery usually takes 3-7 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees and exclude customs, severe weather, and carrier service disruptions.

## Tracking and delays

Tracking may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to show the first carrier scan. If tracking has not changed for 5 business days, support should open a carrier investigation. If an order is more than 7 business days beyond the estimated delivery date, the customer may choose a replacement or refund after investigation.

## Address changes

Shipping addresses can be corrected only before carrier handoff. After dispatch, support may request a carrier intercept, but success is not guaranteed and fees may apply. Orders held for address verification require customer confirmation before fulfillment resumes.
