# Return Policy

## Return window

Most physical products can be returned within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery. The item should be unused, include original accessories, and be packed to prevent transit damage. Final-sale items, opened hygiene products, and activated software licenses are not normally returnable.

## Return workflow

Customers must obtain a return merchandise authorization before shipping an item back. The return label is free when the item is defective, damaged on arrival, or incorrect. For preference-based returns, the customer pays return shipping. Items are inspected after arrival at the warehouse before a refund or replacement is approved.

## Damaged items

Damage or missing parts should be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery with photos of the product and packaging. High-value or safety-related claims are escalated to a human support specialist.
